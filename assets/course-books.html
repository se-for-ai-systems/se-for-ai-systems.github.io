Course Books

Software Engineering for AI Systems

Note
The reading list is not final and may be updated before the course starts.

Required

  • [Bass et al., 2025] L. Bass, Q. Lu, I. Weber, L. Zhu. Engineering AI Systems: Architecture and DevOps Essentials. Addison-Wesley. Referred to as Bass. The main course book: the self-study weeks (2, 4 and 6) follow it section by section.

Recommended

Software architecture

  • [Bass, Clements & Kazman, 2021] L. Bass, P. Clements, R. Kazman. Software Architecture in Practice, 4th ed. Addison-Wesley. Quality-attribute scenarios and tactics.
  • [Brown] S. Brown. The C4 model for visualising software architecture (c4model.com).
  • [Nygard, 2011] M. Nygard. Documenting Architecture Decisions (architecture decision records).
  • [Menzies, 2020] T. Menzies. The Five Laws of SE for AI. IEEE Software 37(1), 81–85. doi:10.1109/MS.2019.2954841.

Machine learning in production

  • [Huyen, 2022] C. Huyen. Designing Machine Learning Systems. O'Reilly. Referred to as Huyen.
  • [Sculley et al., 2015] D. Sculley et al. Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 28 (NeurIPS 2015), 2503–2511.
  • [Kästner, 2025] C. Kästner. Machine Learning in Production: From Models to Products. MIT Press.
  • [Amershi et al., 2019] S. Amershi et al. Software Engineering for Machine Learning: A Case Study. ICSE-SEIP 2019.
  • [Breck et al., 2017] E. Breck, S. Cai, E. Nielsen, M. Salib, D. Sculley. The ML Test Score: A Rubric for ML Production Readiness and Technical Debt Reduction. IEEE Big Data 2017.
  • [Kreuzberger et al., 2023] D. Kreuzberger, N. Kühl, S. Hirschl. Machine Learning Operations (MLOps): Overview, Definition, and Architecture. IEEE Access 11.
  • [Shankar et al., 2022] S. Shankar et al. Operationalizing Machine Learning: An Interview Study. arXiv preprint.
  • [Beyer et al., 2016] B. Beyer et al. Site Reliability Engineering. O'Reilly. Ch. 4 (Service Level Objectives) and 6 (Monitoring Distributed Systems).
  • [OpenGitOps] GitOps Principles v1.0.0 (opengitops.dev).

Knowledge graphs

  • [Hogan et al., 2021] A. Hogan et al. Knowledge Graphs. ACM Computing Surveys 54(4).

Responsible AI and regulation

  • [Mitchell et al., 2019] M. Mitchell et al. Model Cards for Model Reporting. FAT* 2019.
  • [Gebru et al., 2021] T. Gebru et al. Datasheets for Datasets. Communications of the ACM 64(12).
  • [EU, 2024] Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act). In particular Art. 5 (prohibited practices), Art. 6 and Annex III (high-risk systems).
